# RACI — Topics × C-Levels — TRANSITION-ORGANIZATIONS

Derived from the routing table in `transition-organizations-ceo.md`. R = Responsible
(primary), A = Accountable (arbitrates — always the CEO on cross-cutting decisions),
C = Consulted (secondary), I = Informed.

| Topic | R | A | C | I |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy, OKRs, portfolio, agile transformation | cos | CEO | cfo, cino | others |
| Budget, cash, fundraising, valuation, M&A | cfo | CEO | clo, cos | board (if one-way door) |
| Pricing | cfo | CEO | cro, cmo, cpo | — |
| Hiring, firing, org design, compensation, culture | chro | CEO | cfo, clo | holistic-health |
| Sales, pipeline, customers, partnerships | cro | CEO | cmo, cfo | — |
| Brand, demand, communication, crisis | cmo | CEO | cro, clo, csuso | — |
| Contracts, corporate, compliance, risk, privacy | clo (or crco for compliance/enterprise risk if regulated) | CEO | cfo, cto | board (one-way door) |
| Prudential/licensing compliance, regulatory exams, AML/KYC (regulated sectors only) | crco | CEO | clo, cfo | — |
| Operations, quality, efficiency, continuity | coo | CEO | csco, cdaio | — |
| Logistics, procurement, suppliers, S&OP | csco | coo/CEO | csuso, cfo | — |
| Architecture, IT, security, technical debt | cto | CEO | cdaio, clo | — |
| Product, roadmap, discovery, UX | cpo | CEO | cto, cro, csuso | — |
| AI, data, automation, AI governance | cdaio | CEO | cto, clo | all (department ai-automation) |

| Any certification | ccert | CEO | csuso, clo, owning department | — |

| Habits, behavior, trends, market entry | cculture | CEO | cmo, cpo, cino, cro, csuso | — |
| Deep organizational culture, M&A culture merger | cculture | CEO | chro, cos | — |
| Bioeconomy, biomes, peoples, and traditional knowledge | csuso (bioeconomy) | CEO/board | clo, rnd, csco | communities (board) |
| **Every decision (final gate)** | csuso | CEO | — | human user |

**Rule:** the `A` (Accountable) for any cross-cutting decision is always the CEO, except for
one-way doors, where the final approval always rests with the human user, with `board`
deliberation.
