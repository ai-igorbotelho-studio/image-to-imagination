# Global Team Transition Futures — Operating Model

Transition Futures is a boutique foresight practice inside Active Transition. Today it is one
person — Igor Botelho — augmented by a team of Claude Code subagents. This is not a metaphor for
"AI-assisted work"; it is the actual org structure the business runs on until revenue justifies
human hires. Read this file first, then `01-Org-Chart-and-Decision-Rights.md` and
`02-Rollout-Plan-and-KPIs.md`.

## Professional standard, every seat

Every subagent in this org — CEO, Head, and specialist alike — carries a "Professional Standard"
block in its own `.claude/agents/transition-futures/*.md` file: 20+ years of senior practice, a
credential appropriate to the discipline (PhD, MBA, MFA, or Master's, matched to the role — a
foresight PhD for the futures synthesist, an MFA for the brand copywriter, a CPA for the financial
controller), the named methodology it works from (Three Horizons, OKR/OGSM, backward-design
curriculum, WCAG-literate design systems, and so on), and a shared operating mandate: think
systemically, reason strategically, treat AI/automation as a first-class tool for its own research
and drafting, optimize every recommendation for resource efficiency and a named measurable result,
and validate its own output against its stated methodology before handing it off. This is a
standard, not a title — it shapes how each agent reasons and what it checks before answering, not
a credential it name-drops.

## The shape: Igor → CEO → five Heads → specialists

Igor is the founder. He talks to one agent: the **CEO**. The CEO talks to five Heads, never
directly to a specialist, and Igor doesn't brief a Head directly unless he says so explicitly. This
single point of contact is deliberate — it's what lets one founder run a team this size without
personally tracking thirty-odd subagents.

1. **Head of Foresight** — research, signal-scanning, and synthesis. Nine specialist lenses
   (anthropologist, sociologist, consumer-behavior analyst, technology analyst, news & signal
   scout, finance & markets trends analyst, gender analyst, art & culture analyst, design analyst)
   feed the futures synthesist, who names megatrends and scenarios, evidence-traced to ≥2
   independent sources. The portfolio & cause futurist then translates each megatrend into what it
   means for a Family Office versus a Not-for-Profit — Transition Futures' actual whitespace, since
   no competitor in the original 40-firm competitive sweep specializes here.
2. **Head of Portfolio & Delivery** — everything a client actually receives: the brand copywriter's
   editorial narrative, design, creative direction, photography, video, the web build, internal
   systems, and AI-assisted production tooling. This is the production engine behind both product
   lines.
3. **Head of Commercial** — new business development and active prospecting, proposal scoping and
   pricing (engagement architect), account management (client success), and the newsletter/social
   growth engine.
4. **Head of Finance & Ops** — the financial model, pricing discipline, budget, cash flow,
   financial controls, and studio operations, with an independent veto on margin/cash before any
   deal reaches Igor.
5. **Head of Education** — courses, workshops, and multi-touchpoint learning journeys built from
   the same Foresight evidence base, packaged as a distinct product line from Proof Sprint and
   Regenerative Architecture.

Igor sits above all of it as the accountable human — final call on brand voice, pricing, and every
client relationship, because those are exactly the decisions that should NOT be delegated to an AI
agent at this stage of trust and personal reputation.

## What is delegated and what is not

Delegate: research volume, first-draft synthesis, first-draft narrative and creative production,
proposal scoping, internal tooling, course and workshop design.

Never delegate (Igor's exclusive calls): final sign-off on anything sent to a client, pricing,
any claim about a specific person or organization's finances or governance, and the actual
relationship conversation. Agents prepare the ground; Igor closes it.

## How the team works day to day

- **Weekly:** the nine Foresight specialists log findings; the futures synthesist reviews for
  pattern-worthy material; the growth & content manager holds the newsletter/social cadence.
- **Monthly:** the futures synthesist ships or updates the Megatrend Map; the portfolio & cause
  futurist drafts the two segment addenda; the studio director rolls up studio-wide metrics.
- **Per engagement:** business development qualifies → engagement architect scopes and prices
  (checked by the project cost estimator) → Foresight supplies the substance → the brand
  copywriter and the rest of Portfolio & Delivery produce the client-facing package → the CEO does
  the final quality/voice gate → Igor approves and sends.
- **Per education offer:** the Head of Education briefs the curriculum designer, workshop
  facilitator, and learning experience designer against real Foresight material; pricing still
  runs through the engagement architect.
- **Ongoing:** the systems engineer maintains the signal database; the financial controller
  reconciles monthly and reviews AR aging weekly; the client success manager runs account
  check-ins on each client's promised cadence with zero gaps.
