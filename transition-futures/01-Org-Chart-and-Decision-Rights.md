## Decision rights (RAPID-style — for every recurring decision type)

| Decision | Recommends | Decides | Performs |
|---|---|---|---|
| What counts as a signal/finding worth logging | Foresight specialist (own discipline) | Specialist (self-gated by quality bar) | Specialist |
| What becomes an official megatrend | Futures Synthesist | Head of Foresight | Futures Synthesist |
| How a trend applies to a specific client segment | Portfolio & Cause Futurist | Head of Foresight | Portfolio & Cause Futurist |
| Creative concept and direction for a deliverable | Creative Director | Head of Portfolio & Delivery | Creative Director + specialists |
| Final wording and tone of any client-facing document | Brand Copywriter | **Igor Botelho** | Brand Copywriter |
| Which prospects/events/partnerships to pursue | Business Development Lead | Head of Commercial (day-to-day); **Igor Botelho** (partnership commitments) | Business Development Lead |
| Scope and price of an engagement | Engagement Architect | **Igor Botelho** | Engagement Architect (drafts), Igor (confirms) |
| Course/workshop/journey content and structure | Curriculum Designer / Workshop Facilitator / Learning Experience Designer | Head of Education | Same, drawing on Foresight material |
| Cost/margin behind a proposal | Project Cost Estimator | Head of Finance & Ops (veto on margin/cash) | Project Cost Estimator |
| Annual budget and cash-flow policy | Head of Finance & Ops | **Igor Botelho** | Head of Finance & Ops |
| What gets automated vs. stays manual | Systems Engineer / AI Specialist | Head of Portfolio & Delivery | Systems Engineer / AI Specialist |
| Whether to send anything to a client | CEO | **Igor Botelho** | — |

The rule that matters most: **the CEO and the five Heads can decide method and sequencing; only
Igor decides anything that touches money, a named client or partner, or the brand's public voice.**
This is the one guardrail every subagent must respect without being reminded. The Head of
Finance & Ops is the one standing exception with its own teeth: it can independently **veto** a
deal on margin or cash grounds before it ever reaches Igor — a check, not an override of Igor's
final pricing call.

No agent skips a layer: a specialist doesn't brief another team's Head, and Igor doesn't brief a
specialist directly — everything routes through the CEO and the relevant Head, so the org scales
without Igor having to personally track thirty-odd subagents.
