# Rollout Plan and KPIs

## Why phase it instead of standing up all thirty-odd agents on day one

Scaling a team before the delivery model is proven is the most expensive and most common mistake
in early-stage operations — it multiplies a process that doesn't work yet instead of a process
that does. Transition Futures has no paying client yet, so the team is sequenced to prove the
Proof Sprint pipeline first, sell from that proof, and only then build out the recurring-retainer
and education infrastructure.

## Phase 1 — Prove the pipeline (before any client is signed)

Stand up: **CEO, Head of Foresight** (with the core research lenses: News & Signal Scout,
Futures Synthesist, plus Anthropologist and Consumer Behavior Analyst to start — add the remaining
five Foresight specialists as questions demand them), **Head of Portfolio & Delivery** (with
Brand Copywriter active; Designer, Creative Director, Photographer, Videomaker, Web Developer, AI
Specialist on standby until a deliverable needs them), **Head of Commercial, Head of Finance &
Ops.**

This is the minimum chain that can turn a scan into a shippable Proof Sprint narrative — plus the
CEO who coordinates it all, and the two functional Heads (Commercial, Finance & Ops) whose job
costs no delivery capacity and needs to start on day one: Commercial builds the prospecting
pipeline and the events/partnerships map before there's anything to scope, and Finance & Ops gets
the cost/margin discipline in place before the first proposal exists. Goal: produce one complete,
unpaid demonstration Proof Sprint end-to-end, in under 10 business days, in the intended voice.

## Phase 2 — Once the first real proposal goes out

Add: **Portfolio & Cause Futurist, Project Cost Estimator, Client Success Manager, Growth &
Content Manager,** and the remaining **Foresight specialists** (Sociologist, Technology Analyst,
Finance & Markets Trends Analyst, Gender Analyst, Art & Culture Analyst, Design Analyst) as the
questions the practice is fielding actually call for each lens.

Trigger to move to this phase: Igor has a real brief in hand (not hypothetical) and needs it
scoped, priced, and segment-translated professionally rather than ad hoc. Project Cost Estimator
comes in alongside Engagement Architect (already active in Phase 1 under Commercial) so every
proposal carries a real margin check from the start. Client Success Manager stands up the moment
the first client is signed — punctuality on delivery 1 is the whole reputation. Growth & Content
Manager starts running the newsletter/social funnel once there's a first real story worth
distributing.

## Phase 3 — Once a Regenerative Architecture retainer is sold

Add: **Systems Engineer, Business Development Lead's new-product function, Financial Controller,
Studio Director, FP&A Analyst, Designer, Creative Director, Photographer, Videomaker, Web
Developer, AI Specialist** (the full Portfolio & Delivery bench).

Trigger to move to this phase: a client has actually bought the ongoing-monitoring product, not
just the one-off report. Building the monitoring system, or staffing a full production bench,
before anyone is paying for it is capacity built ahead of demand — avoid it. Before this phase,
the Brand Copywriter alone carries Portfolio & Delivery, and Finance & Ops / Commercial carry their
functions at a lighter weight.

## Phase 4 — Once Education is a validated product

Add: **Head of Education, Curriculum Designer, Workshop Facilitator, Learning Experience
Designer.**

Trigger to move to this phase: a real prospect or client has explicitly asked for a course,
workshop, or learning journey — not a hypothetical market opportunity. Education draws on the
same Foresight evidence base and the same Brand Copywriter, so it is deliberately the last product
line stood up, once the foresight-to-delivery pipeline is already proven twice over.

## KPIs — exactly as Igor defined them, not redefined here

**North Star:** conversion rate, tracked at two layers:
1. Proposal conversion by segment — Family Office vs. NFP vs. secondary audiences.
2. First-conversation → proposal conversion.

Both are currently **`[baseline not yet established — set after the first 10 real
first-conversations]`.** Do not invent a target number; log real data from the first 10
conversations, then set a target with Igor.

**Counter-metrics (to prevent optimizing conversion at the cost of what makes Transition Futures
worth converting to):**
- Zero brand-voice violations in client-facing material (the CEO's quality gate, checked against
  the Brand Copywriter's voice rules).
- 100% of synthesis claims traceable to at least two independent Foresight sources (no
  single-source trend calls reaching a client).

**Review cadence:** weekly for signal volume and pipeline health; monthly for the Megatrend Map and
conversion numbers; quarterly for whether to advance to the next rollout phase.
