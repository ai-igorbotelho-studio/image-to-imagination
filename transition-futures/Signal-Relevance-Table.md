# Signal Relevance Table

This table links each megatrend to the segments and product lines it matters for. Every megatrend
comes from the futures synthesist. It is traced to at least 2 independent signals, and the Head of
Foresight has approved it. The portfolio & cause futurist maintains this table. The engagement
architect, on the transition-futures-head-of-commercial's team, reads segment relevance from here
when scoping a proposal and does not guess at it.

## Who updates it and when

- **Maintained by:** transition-futures-portfolio-cause-futurist. The Head of Foresight decides how
  a trend applies to a client segment, as set out in `01-Org-Chart-and-Decision-Rights.md`.
- **Update when:** the futures synthesist names a new megatrend or revises one, or the evidence
  behind a row changes. Also re-check every row before a proposal goes out that relies on it.
- **Read by:** transition-futures-engagement-architect, while scoping a Proof Sprint or a
  Regenerative Architecture retainer.
- **Never in this table:** a named client's portfolio or cause specifics, and nothing about
  pricing or brand voice. Those are escalated, and pricing is Igor's call. Rows are about segments.
  They do not describe individual clients.
- **Single-source rule:** a signal that has only one source does not get a row. Log it with the
  specialist who found it until a second independent signal supports it.

## Scoring key

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 3 | High. It changes what the engagement is about, so lead with it. |
| 2 | Medium. Include it as a supporting theme or scenario input. |
| 1 | Low. Mention it only if the client raises it. |
| 0 | Not relevant for this segment or product line. |

**FO** = Family Office · **NFP** = Not-for-Profit · **PS** = Proof Sprint (fast, tactical,
weeks-long) · **RA** = Regenerative Architecture (retainer with a named cadence)

## Adding a row

1. Copy the megatrend name exactly as the synthesist named it.
2. List at least 2 independent signals with their source lens. If you have only one, leave the row out.
3. Score FO, NFP, PS and RA from 0 to 3 using the scoring key. When the reason for a score is not
   obvious, give it in the notes.
4. Write the FO-versus-NFP translation in one or two sentences. Keep it about segments, never a
   named client.
5. Date the row. Rows not reviewed in the last two quarters should be re-checked before any
   proposal cites them.
