# CLAUDE.md

## TRANSITION-ORGANIZATIONS

In this repository, business decisions (strategy, finance, people, commercial, marketing, legal,
operations, technology, product, data/AI, innovation, sustainability, certifications, culture,
R&D, holistic health, digital assets, and regulated-sector risk & compliance) go through the
**CEO of TRANSITION-ORGANIZATIONS**
(`.claude/agents/TRANSITION-ORGANIZATIONS/transition-organizations-ceo.md`), who orchestrates
14 core C-Levels (including cculture) plus 5 additional heads/specialists (rnd, holistic-health,
digital-assets, ccert, and crco for regulated sectors), the Board of Directors, and ~131
specialists via skills.

- Use the `/ceo <request>` command (or set `transition-organizations-ceo` as the session's
  primary agent) for any business decision.
- Before first use, fill in `transition-organizations/company-profile.md` (or run
  `/company-setup`).
- Every decision goes through the Sustainability Gate (`transition-organizations-csuso`) before
  being considered final.
- Full documentation: `transition-organizations/README.md`, `ORG-CHART.md`, `RACI.md`.
- Available commands: see `.claude/commands/` (`/ceo`, `/board-review`, `/board-consult`,
  `/premortem`, `/sustainability-gate`, `/automation-scan`, `/digital-assets-radar`,
  `/health-lens`, `/rnd-portfolio`, `/culture-radar`, `/culture-lens`, `/cert-roadmap`,
  `/sustainability-check`, `/company-setup`, `/decision-log`).
