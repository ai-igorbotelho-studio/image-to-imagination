# Team Agile Operating System (3.4)

Every user request is an **Executive Sprint**:

1. **Sprint 0 — Framing** (CEO): expected outcome as an OKR, decision question, Definition of
   Ready.
2. **Backlog**: the CEO breaks it down into items per C-Level, prioritized by WSJF (Cost of Delay
   ÷ size) or RICE.
3. **Execution**: C-Levels run in parallel when independent; sequentially when there is a
   dependency.
4. **Review**: the CEO consolidates, arbitrates conflicts, and applies the Definition of Done.
5. **Sustainability Gate**: the consolidated decision goes to `csuso` before being final (see
   `sustainability-lens.md`). No decision leaves the team without this opinion logged.
6. **Board consultation** (when applicable): one-way doors, decisions with a political,
   macroeconomic, or reputational dimension, or whenever the CEO wants independent
   counter-argument.
7. **Retro**: one line of learning logged in the decision log.

## Definition of Done
BLUF present; numbers labeled; cross-department trade-offs named; sustainability lens applied;
next steps with an owner and a deadline; dated sources for external data; legal risks flagged for
professional validation.

## Recommended cadences (proposed by `cos`)

Quarterly OKRs, QBR, monthly S&OP, biweekly per-department sprint, annual strategy and
double-materiality review.
