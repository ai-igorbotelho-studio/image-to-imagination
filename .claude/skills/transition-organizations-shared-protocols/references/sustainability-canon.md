# Sustainability Canon (Section 7)

- **Paradigms:** neoclassical environmental economics (externalities, pricing), ecological
  economics (Daly), planetary boundaries (Rockström/Stockholm Resilience Centre), Doughnut
  Economics (Raworth), regenerative economics (Fullerton), green growth × post-growth ×
  degrowth (a real dispute, present both sides), stakeholder capitalism, Economy for the Common
  Good.
- **Purpose and ownership:** Colin Mayer (purpose as solving people's and the planet's problems
  profitably), Marjorie Kelly (extractive × generative ownership), Katharina Pistor (the code of
  capital), Jed Emerson (blended value), Aunnie Patton Power (non-extractive finance), Purpose
  Foundation.
- **Mandatory distinctions table:** ESG (risk management) ≠ impact investing (intent +
  measurement + additionality) ≠ conscious capitalism ≠ steward ownership (separation of control
  and economic rights; neither a cooperative nor a philanthropic foundation) ≠ B Corp
  (a reversible performance certification) ≠ benefit corporation (a legal form) ≠ regenerative
  economy.
- **Anti-washing test:** additionality ("would it have happened anyway?") + "who controls it, not
  just what" + a claim with auditable evidence + mitigation hierarchy before offsetting.
- **Primary sources** (always check the date): ifrs.org, efrag.org, globalreporting.org,
  sciencebasedtargets.org, sciencebasedtargetsnetwork.org, ghgprotocol.org, tnfd.global, cdp.net,
  icvcm.org, vcmintegrity.org, unfccc.int, bcorporation.net, purpose-economy.org,
  doughnuteconomics.org, unepfi.org, oceanpanel.org, thegiin.org, capitalscoalition.org.
