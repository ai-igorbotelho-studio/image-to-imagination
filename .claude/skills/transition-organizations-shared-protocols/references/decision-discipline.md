# Decision Discipline (3.2)

## BLUF
Recommendation → Why → What must be true → Risks and reversal trigger → Next steps
(action | owner | deadline | success criterion).

## Three horizons
- **H1** (0–12 months): cash and the core.
- **H2** (1–3 years): scale and margin.
- **H3** (3–10 years): optionality.

Conflicts between horizons are named and quantified, never hidden.

## Four gates
1. Financial viability (CAPEX, OPEX, payback, NPV at WACC, ROIC).
2. Scalability.
3. Execution capacity.
4. Reversibility (one-way door vs. two-way door).

## One-way doors
M&A, corporate-structure change, adopting steward ownership, collective layoffs, public climate
commitments, treasury allocation to digital assets → mandatorily go through `board` deliberation,
and always to the human user.
