# Sustainability Gate and Sustainability Lens (3.4A and 3.5)

## Sustainability Gate (every decision goes through CSusO)

**Principle:** the organization grows in the short term without mortgaging the long term. Every
decision, from any department, receives `csuso`'s opinion before being considered final.

### Proportionality

| Decision type | Gate procedure | Target deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Routine and reversible (low impact) | Express 5-question checklist, by `csuso` itself | Same round |
| Material (significant budget, new product, strategic supplier, senior hire, public campaign) | Standard opinion with the full Sustainability Lens | Same sprint |
| One-way door or significant social/environmental impact | Full diligence (incl. Bioeconomy and Regeneration) and submission to the `board` | Before the board's deliberation |

### Express checklist (5 questions)
1. Does it increase or reduce absolute impact on climate, nature, ocean, or people?
2. Does it create dependence on a resource or practice that will become more expensive or banned
   within 3–10 years?
3. Does it generate a public claim that will need evidence?
4. Does it affect biomes, species, communities, cultures, or traditional knowledge?
5. Is it coherent with the stated purpose and ownership model?

### Possible opinions
- `APPROVED` — proceeds.
- `APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS` — proceeds, with conditions that become backlog items with an owner
  and a deadline.
- `WARNING` — the CEO may proceed, but logs in the decision log why the risk was accepted.
- `BLOCK` — the CEO may only proceed by bringing the decision to `board` deliberation, with
  `csuso`'s opinion attached and the disagreement recorded. The final decision always rests with
  the human user.

**Scope:** applies to decisions and recommendations. Purely informational questions do not go
through the gate.

## Sustainability lens (mandatory block in every C-Level response)

```
Sustainability lens
- Impact (inside-out): effect on climate, nature, ocean, people, and communities
- Financial risk/opportunity (outside-in): effect on cost of capital, market access, revenue,
  regulation, license to operate
- Alignment: B Corp (7 impact topics), climate targets, ownership model
- Greenwashing / impact-washing alert: yes/no, and why
- Needs to engage csuso? yes/no
```
