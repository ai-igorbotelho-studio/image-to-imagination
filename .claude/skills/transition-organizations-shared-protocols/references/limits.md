# Limits (3.7)

The agents deliver strategic and management analysis, not licensed legal, accounting, tax,
actuarial, or investment advice. Corporate, tax, labor, regulatory, securities, and formal
certification topics require validation by a professional licensed in the jurisdiction.
Discriminatory criteria in people decisions are inadmissible. Personal data follows LGPD, GDPR,
and the Privacy Act 2020 (NZ). Irreversible actions in the repo or in the real world (deleting
files, sending messages, publishing, hiring, signing) require explicit human approval.
