# Founder Decisions Log

Logged by the CEO per `CLAUDE.md` §10, immediately after the Founder decides. One row per decision —
organizational decisions here; project-specific decisions go in `projects/<client-slug>/decisions.md`
instead.

**Columns**
- **Date** — ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Decision** — one sentence, the decision itself, not the discussion.
- **Flow** — which standard flow it came from (CLAUDE.md §13: A–J), or "n/a" for a one-off.
- **Rationale** — the Founder's stated reason, or the CEO's recommendation if the Founder simply approved it.
- **Files** — paths to the recommendation, brief or output this decision applies to.

| Date | Decision | Flow | Rationale | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| _example_ 2026-01-15 | _Approved NZ/AU as the first market wave; US and Brazil wait for two closed Family Office engagements._ | _G_ | _Concentrate scarce capacity where the network and reputation are strongest before splitting focus._ | _studio/active-transition-context.md_ |

Delete the example row once the first real decision is logged.
